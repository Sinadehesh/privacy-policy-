Privacy Policy

DeBubble for Android

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeBubble (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in the DeBubble app for Android (the “App”). DeBubble is a self-care app: small daily tasks for your body, your room and your heart, and small steps outside your comfort zone, shown as a pixel-art room that grows as you go.

Contact: sinadehesh@gmail.com

The short version. Everything you put into DeBubble stays on your phone. The App has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere. We never see it.

1. What the App keeps, and where

To work, the App keeps what you give it. All of it is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It includes:

  • your answers during setup, such as how you usually get around, how far from home you tend to go, how much you want to spend on activities, and the habits you said you would like to change;
  • the routines you choose, and the cues you write for them (for example, “When I put the kettle on”);
  • your check-ins on how your body, room and heart are doing, and how a task felt afterwards;
  • the tasks and missions you have done or tried, and what you noted when one did not go to plan;
  • your guesses before a mission and how it actually went;
  • the journal notes (“postcards”) you write;
  • the reminder times you set;
  • your character's look, the items in your room, your progress and your settings.

Some of this is personal, and some of it may relate to how you feel. That is why it never leaves your device through the App.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data from the App. The App does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and does not request the internet permission.

3. Permissions

The App asks Android for only these permissions:

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set yourself. The App asks the first time you set a reminder, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Run at startup: so the reminders you set still go off after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for small haptic feedback when you complete something.

The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other app.

4. Android backup

If you have turned on backup on your phone, Android may include the App's data in your device backup, stored in your Google account, and may copy it when you move to a new phone. This lets you keep your progress. The backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

5. Your home screen and notifications

If you add the DeBubble widget, it shows your room and today's tasks on your home screen, and reminders appear as notifications. Anyone who can see your screen may see them. You can remove the widget, and choose how notifications appear on your lock screen, in your phone's settings.

6. Google Play

When you download the App from Google Play, Google processes information about that under the Google Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with app developers, Google may give us aggregated statistics and crash reports, such as how many people installed the App and technical details of a crash. These do not include anything you wrote or recorded in the App.

7. Deleting your information

You can delete a journal note inside the App. To delete everything, uninstall the App, or go to your phone's Settings, then Apps, then DeBubble, then Storage, and clear its storage. Because we hold none of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Information stays on your device until you delete it.

8. Security

The App's data is kept in private storage that Android protects from other apps. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the App, so we recommend using a screen lock.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Since your information stays on your device and under your control, you can see, change or delete it in the App or as described above. If you have any question about your data, contact us at sinadehesh@gmail.com.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included.

11. Changes to this policy

If the App ever starts handling information differently (for example, if purchases are added through Google Play), we will update this policy before that change reaches the App, post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.
